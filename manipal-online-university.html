Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Manipal Online programs, fees, placement and admissions.

Is Manipal Online degree valid?
Yes, Manipal Online degrees are UGC-entitled and valid for jobs and higher education in India.
What is the fee for Manipal MCA online?
The total fee for Manipal Online MCA is approximately ₹1.50 – ₹1.80 lakhs (may vary by batch).
What is the fees of Manipal Online MBA?
The total fee for Manipal Online MBA is approximately ₹1.60 – ₹1.90 lakhs (may vary).
Does Manipal Online provide placement?
Yes, Manipal Online offers placement assistance through career support services, but not a 100% placement guarantee.
Can I join Manipal without entrance exam?
For many online programs, entrance exams are not required. Eligibility is based on academic qualification (e.g. 10+2 or graduation marks).
How long is Manipal Online MBA program?
Manipal Online MBA is a 2-year postgraduate program designed for working professionals.
Is Manipal Online MBA valid?
Yes, Manipal Online MBA is UGC-entitled and valid in India for employment and further studies.
What is the total cost of an MBA at Manipal?
Manipal Online MBA costs around ₹1.60 – ₹1.90 lakhs. Regular campus MBA fees are considerably higher.
