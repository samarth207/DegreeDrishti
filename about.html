About DegreeDrishti

Empowering Students with Quality Online Education Since 2018

Our Story

DegreeDrishti was founded with a simple yet powerful mission: to make quality higher education accessible to everyone, regardless of their location or circumstances. What started as a small initiative has now grown into India's most trusted online education platform.

We believe that education should not be limited by geographical boundaries or financial constraints. Our platform bridges the gap between students and top-tier universities, offering UGC-approved online and distance learning programs that are recognized globally.

With over 50,000 successful admissions and counting, we have helped students from all walks of life achieve their educational dreams and career aspirations.

Our Mission

To democratize education by providing affordable, accessible, and quality online learning opportunities that empower individuals to transform their careers and lives.

Our Vision

To become the leading online education platform in India, known for our commitment to student success, innovation in learning, and partnerships with world-class universities.

Our Values

Integrity, student-first approach, continuous improvement, and building lasting relationships with students and university partners.
