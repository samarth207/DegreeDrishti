Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Amity Online programs, fees, scholarships and placements.

Is Amity Online degree valid in UAE?
Yes, Amity Online degrees are valid if attested properly. For UAE jobs, you may need MOFA or embassy attestation as per employer requirement.
Is Amity Online degree valid?
Yes, Amity Online degrees are UGC-entitled and valid in India for jobs and higher studies.
Does Amity Online offer scholarships?
Yes, Amity Online offers scholarships for meritorious students, defense personnel, differently-abled students, and others as per eligibility.
How to get a scholarship in Amity?
You need to apply during admission and submit required documents. Scholarship approval depends on eligibility criteria.
Does Amity have 100% placement?
Amity provides placement assistance, but 100% placement guarantee is not officially assured. It depends on student performance.
What is the cost of Amity Online MBA?
The total fee for Amity Online MBA is approximately ₹1.80 – ₹2.00 lakhs (may vary by specialisation and batch).
Is online MBA worth it from Amity?
Yes, if you want flexible learning with a UGC-approved degree and career support, Amity Online MBA is a solid choice for working professionals.
Is Amity University good for online BBA?
Yes, Amity Online BBA offers a structured curriculum, flexible classes, and a UGC-entitled degree recognised across India.
What is the fee for Amity MCA online?
The total fee for Amity Online MCA is approximately ₹1.70 – ₹1.90 lakhs (may vary by batch).
