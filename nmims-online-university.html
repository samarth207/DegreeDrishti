Yes, all NMIMS Online degrees are UGC-approved and recognized by employers across India and globally. NMIMS is a NAAC A++ accredited university.

Yes, NMIMS Online programs are designed for working professionals. You can continue your job while pursuing your degree with flexible weekend classes and online learning.
